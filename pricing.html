Frequently Asked Questions

What are the limits on the free self-hosted version?

10,000 verifications per month, 2 agents, and 30-day audit retention. Upgrade to Self-Hosted Pro for unlimited everything.

How do you enforce limits on self-hosted?

The proxy periodically checks a local usage counter against the license key. Free tier runs fully offline — Pro tier needs occasional online validation but works offline for 30 days.

Does the managed cloud touch my data?

If you use our managed cloud, verification requests pass through our proxy. We do not store your source documents or LLM responses beyond the audit chain metadata. For complete data sovereignty, use the self-hosted version.

Can I get a trial of the managed cloud?

Yes — buy a starter pack and get 10,000 verifications. No commitments.
